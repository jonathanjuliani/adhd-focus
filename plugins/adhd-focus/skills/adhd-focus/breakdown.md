# Breakdown shapes

Read this only after the reader has asked for a breakdown. Pick the one shape that fits the request. Every shape still opens with the answer or the first action and still ends with one next action.

Never invent owners, dates, commitments, or test results. Mark anything uncertain "needs confirmation".

## Plan a project or a vague request

If the scope is unclear, ask up to three questions, one at a time. Then produce:

```markdown
## Brief
Objective · Who it is for · Constraints · Non-goals · Definition of done · Risks and unknowns

## Tasks (15 to 90 minutes each)
| # | Task (verb + outcome) | First physical action | Verify by | Estimate |

## Do first today
<one task>

## Confirm with a human
<assumptions to check before building>
```

A task with no physical first action is still a question or a decision. List it under "Confirm with a human" instead.

## Explain a concept

One-sentence answer, then headers, then one concrete example, then "Try: <one action>".

## Estimate

Best, likely, and worst case in units. Then the biggest unknown, and what would shrink the range.

## Overwhelm or too many ideas

Capture everything, then sort into **Now** (one to three), **Later**, **Delegate**, and **Drop**. End with one action that takes ten minutes or less.

## Lost context or resuming work

Four lines: goal, current state, blocker, next action.

## Work-session partner

Session goal, timebox, three checkpoints, and a "not doing now" list. At each checkpoint ask: what got done, what is the next physical action, and is the work still on the agreed goal. New work that arrives mid-session is classified as do now, do later, delegate, or drop.

## Communication artifacts

- **Status update:** status (on track, at risk, or blocked), done since last update, in progress with expected date, next, blockers (what is needed, from whom, by when), risk and mitigation.
- **Meeting to actions:** a three-bullet TL;DR, decisions, open questions with who answers each, actions with owner, date and done-when, risks, and a ready-to-send update. Under 300 words unless asked for more.
- **Clarification request:** my understanding, up to three open questions, a proposed definition of done, and "If I do not hear back by <date>, I will proceed with <assumption>."
- **Deadline risk:** current estimate as a range against the original expectation, the main uncertainty, the options (cut scope, extend, add help, ship a smaller first version), and a recommendation.
- **PR description:** problem, solution, user-visible behavior, risks and trade-offs, test plan, rollback. Never claim a test passed unless the reader said so.
- **Weekly review:** what moved forward, what stalled and its real blocker, decisions to communicate, what to drop, delegate, or defer, and next week as must, should, could, and not doing.
